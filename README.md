#Bakery Sales Performance Analysis
#Project Overview
This project analyzes bakery sales data to understand revenue, profitability, customer purchasing patterns, seasonal performance and inventory waste.

The objective is to generate actionable business insights that support better production planning, inventory management and revenue optimization.

#Tools Used
Microsoft Excel
Python
MySQL
Microsoft Power BI
Business Objectives
Evaluate total revenue and profitability.
Identify the best-performing sales days.
Analyze revenue by product category.
Compare seasonal production with units sold.
Examine revenue by payment method.
Investigate unsold inventory across weather conditions.
Analyze festival-related sales patterns.
